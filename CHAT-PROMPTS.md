## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be Specific with Timing**
   - ✅ "Add text at 15 seconds"
   - ❌ "Add text here"

2. **Reference Elements Clearly**
   - ✅ "Change the intro card title"
   - ❌ "Change the title"

3. **Use Exact Values**
   - ✅ "Make it 60 seconds, blue (#3b82f6)"
   - ❌ "Make it longer and blue-ish"

4. **Combine Related Edits**
   - ✅ "Add lower third 'John Doe' at 5s, blue accent, 8s duration"
   - ❌ Three separate messages

5. **Describe the Feel**
   - ✅ "Make it feel premium with darker colors and slower transitions"
   - ❌ "Make it better"
